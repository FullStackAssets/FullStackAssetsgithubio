How the math works

  • Food cost % = food cost per plate ÷ menu price × 100
  • Margin $ = menu price − food cost per plate
  • Waste-adjusted cost = food cost ÷ (1 − waste %)
  • Price for target food-cost % = food cost ÷ target %

Example, checked: $18.00 price, $5.40 food cost, 10% waste → food cost 30.0%, margin $12.60, waste-adjusted cost $6.00, waste-adjusted food cost 33.3%. Target 28% on $5.40 → $19.29.
