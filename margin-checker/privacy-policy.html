Margin Checker — Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy describes Margin Checker v1: free, without advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, accounts, or an app-owned backend.

What Margin Checker does

Margin Checker is a restaurant dish margin calculator. You enter a menu price, ingredient quantities and costs, a waste percentage, and a target food-cost percentage. It calculates food cost per plate, food-cost percentage, margin, waste-adjusted cost and margin, a target price, and an optional daily margin estimate. It also offers an optional public FDA recall search.

Data stored on your device only

Dishes you save are stored only on your device, using local browser/app storage. Margin Checker has no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your dish names, prices, ingredient costs, or saved dishes. Deleting saved dishes in the app removes them from that device. Clearing the app's data also removes them.

FDA recall search

If you use the recall search, your search words (for example, an ingredient word, an optional state code, and an optional classification) are sent directly to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration's public openFDA API at api.fda.gov, so it can return matching public recall records. The app does not include dish names, menu prices, ingredient costs, account identifiers, or contact information in its search parameters. As with any internet request, the FDA service and network intermediaries may receive connection metadata such as an IP address. Recall results are FDA public enforcement records, updated periodically and potentially incomplete or delayed. They are not live safety notifications, vendor data, or pricing, and they are not a guarantee of product safety. Check official FDA recall notices before acting.

Links to other sites

The app links to HostGraph Procurement Command Center and MarginLeak, which are separate products with their own sites and policies. Tapping those links does not share your dish data with them. If you use those sites, their policies apply to that use.

What we do not collect in version 1

  • No account, name, email, or contact information is collected by the app itself.
  • No advertising identifier, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, or tracking is included in version 1.
  • No location data is collected.
  • No bank, payment, or credential information is requested or collected by the app.

If ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or any account feature are added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy answers will be updated to match.

Children

Margin Checker is a business calculator for restaurant operators. It is not directed at children.
