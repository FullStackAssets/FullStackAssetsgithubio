Margin Checker — Support

Support information for Margin Checker v1 (free, without advertising or in-app purchases).

What Margin Checker does

Price one dish at a time: enter a menu price, ingredient quantity × unit cost, waste %, and target food-cost %. The app shows food cost per plate, food-cost %, margin $, waste-adjusted cost and margin, the price needed for your target %, and, if you enter plates per day, a daily margin estimate before labour, rent, and other overhead.

Saved dishes stay on your device only. There is no account and no server.

Quick start

  1. Enter a dish name, menu price, and waste %.
  2. Add ingredients: quantity per plate × cost per unit. Tap "Calculate margin".
  3. Tap "Save dish on this device" to keep it. Tap "Load" on a saved dish to bring it back.
  4. Tap "Load SAMPLE dish" to see a worked example: $18.00 price, $5.40 food cost, 10% waste = 30.0% food cost, $12.60 margin, $6.00 waste-adjusted cost, 33.3% waste-adjusted. Replace the sample with your own costs before making a pricing decision.

Recall search

Enter an ingredient word and, optionally, a state code and classification, then search. Results come from the FDA's public openFDA database and are identified as public enforcement records, not live safety notifications. They are not vendor data and not prices. If a search returns nothing, it may mean no matching public recall record, or the FDA service may be temporarily unavailable — the app will say so. Check the FDA record itself before acting.

Troubleshooting

  • Calculator shows $0: check that at least one ingredient has both a quantity and a unit cost, and that the menu price is greater than zero.
  • Saved dish is gone: saved dishes live only on that device. Clearing app data, reinstalling, or switching devices will remove them. There is no cloud backup in version 1.
  • Recall search fails: the calculator still works offline. Recall search needs an internet connection to api.fda.gov. Try again, or check api.fda.gov directly.
  • Numbers look wrong: check waste % — waste-adjusted cost is food cost ÷ (1 − waste %). A 10% waste rate raises a $5.40 cost to $6.00, not by 10% of the price.

What this app is not

Not financial advice, not accounting advice, and not a food-safety certification. Margin $ in the app is before labour, rent, utilities, and other overhead unless you add those costs yourself as ingredients/overhead lines in your own calculation.
